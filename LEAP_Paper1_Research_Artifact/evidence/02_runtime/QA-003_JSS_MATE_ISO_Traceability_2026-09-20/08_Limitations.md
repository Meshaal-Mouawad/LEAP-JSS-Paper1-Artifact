# Limitations

- MATE tests establish deterministic rendering and source-to-output traceability; they do not measure formula accuracy against an adjudicated gold set.
- ISO 22400 tests cover two configured templates only: yield efficiency and asset utilization.
- The ISO tests do not establish certification, conformance of an enterprise implementation, or exhaustive ISO 22400 coverage.
- The 38 controlled scenarios are behavioral tests, not population-level extraction precision or recall evidence.
